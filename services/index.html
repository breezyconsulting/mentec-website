Services

Every engagement is tailored. This is the full range we draw from.

Mentec doesn't sell a fixed package. Each partner gets a program of works assembled from these six areas — starting wherever the business actually needs it.

CFO — 01

CFO Leadership

Best for: businesses making real decisions without a finance voice in the room

On-call, senior financial leadership without carrying a full-time seat. A member of the Australian Society of Certified Professional Accountants with 30+ years of experience, sitting at the table when it matters.

  • Board packs and management reporting on a regular cadence
  • Lender and investor-ready reporting when capital conversations start
  • Cash flow forecasting and KPI dashboards
  • A senior financial voice in commercial decisions as they're being made, not after
STR — 02

Strategic Planning

Best for: a strong business without a clear, sequenced plan to scale it

A corporate strategy built around your specific commercial model — not a generic template — then translated into a plan the business can actually deliver.

  • Initial due diligence and review of current processes and documents
  • A high-level program agreed with all parties before work begins
  • Translation of strategy into simple, deliverable project plans
  • Clear alignment on what "success" needs to mean for this business
FIN — 03

Financial Management

Best for: businesses where growth is starting to outrun cash discipline

Financial analysis and systems that optimise how the business's resources are deployed, so ambition doesn't outpace the numbers behind it.

  • Resource allocation and margin analysis by project or business line
  • Cash flow management and working capital discipline
  • Financial systems and reporting cadence set up to scale with the business
  • Ongoing financial development toward a more resilient base
EXE — 04

Execution & Delivery

Best for: businesses that already have a plan sitting on a shelf

The part most advisory firms skip. Mentec works inside the business to drive delivery of the plan — not just recommend it.

  • Working within the business to drive delivery of the agreed plan
  • Progress reporting to all parties on a regular rhythm
  • Course-correction as real conditions test the plan
  • Accountability that doesn't end when the strategy document is signed off
VAL — 05

Enterprise Value & Growth

Best for: owners thinking about what the business is actually worth

Identifying and sequencing the moves that increase what the business is worth — not just what it earns this year.

  • A read on where enterprise value is being created or quietly lost
  • Prioritisation of growth moves by value impact, not just revenue
  • Positioning the business for a future raise, sale, or succession, if relevant
  • Learning from present and past experience to set a repeatable blueprint
DD — 06

Due Diligence & Risk

Best for: businesses about to take on a partner, lender, or major commitment

Initial due diligence, process and document review, with risk flagged early — before it becomes an expensive surprise.

  • Document and process review across the business
  • Risk identification ahead of a funding, partnership, or growth decision
  • Plain-language findings, not a compliance report nobody reads
  • A clear view of what needs fixing before it's someone else's discovery
