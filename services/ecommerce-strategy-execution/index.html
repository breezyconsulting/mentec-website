Ecommerce Strategy & Execution

Ecommerce strategy built on unit economics, not just traffic.

Channel growth that doesn't hold together commercially isn't growth — it's deferred risk. This service keeps the two in the same room.

What this covers

Channel growth that holds together commercially.

Channel growth that doesn't hold together commercially isn't growth — it's deferred risk. This service keeps strategy and unit economics in the same room.

  • Channel and marketplace strategy sequenced by margin and capacity, not just opportunity size
  • Unit economics and contribution margin by channel, product and cohort
  • Conversion, retention and lifetime value roadmap tied to the financial plan
  • Integration with cash flow and reporting, so growth doesn't outrun working capital

“This capability is led by people who have held senior positions across multiple sectors, delivering real growth for both private and publicly listed companies — judgment that's been tested against real P&Ls, not just frameworks.”
