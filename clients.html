Clients

Who we partner with, and why we're selective about it.

Mentec's model only works when both sides are genuinely aligned. That starts with taking on the right partners in the first place.

Fit

Mentec partners typically have:

  • An innovative or genuinely unique idea, product or service
  • A strong, proven commercial model already generating revenue
  • SME scale — established enough to have real financial complexity, not yet large enough to justify a full-time CFO
  • A continued drive to grow, not just maintain
